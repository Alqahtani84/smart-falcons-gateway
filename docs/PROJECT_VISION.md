# Project Vision — Smart Falcons Gateway

## What it is
An integrated **falcon-only** identity and lifecycle platform for the Saudi/Gulf context.
The platform is exclusively for falcons — it is **not** generalized to other birds, animals,
pets, or wildlife.

## Long-term capabilities (the 15-feature vision)
1. Falcon eye-biometric identification
2. Digital Falcon Passport
3. Falcon registry and ownership records
4. GPS tracking and geofencing
5. Health and veterinary records
6. Digital Twin for each falcon (structured lifecycle representation, not merely a 3D model)
7. AI-based readiness, risk, and performance indicators
8. Genealogy and breeding records
9. Competition and race records
10. Ownership-transfer workflows
11. Alerts and notifications
12. Government dashboards
13. Investor and commercial dashboards
14. Reporting and analytics
15. Arabic and English interfaces (RTL + LTR)

## The one thing that gates everything
The platform's value depends on an **unproven scientific question**:

> **Can the falcon eye region serve as a reliable biometric identifier?**

If it cannot, most of the vision (passport identity, registry binding, ownership proof, anti-fraud)
loses its anchor. Therefore we **prove feasibility before building the platform.**

## Strategy: prove first, build second
- **Feasibility Phase (now):** Track A (screen existing images + build the eye-preprocessing
  pipeline) and Track B (controlled pilot of known falcons across ≥2 sessions). A formal
  **GO / NO-GO / PIVOT** decision precedes any platform work.
- **Platform Phase (gated):** the 15 features are delivered in phases A–K, each with its own
  decision gate, only after a biometric GO.

## Non-negotiable context
- **Deployment:** Saudi Arabia; design for Arabic RTL + English, privacy, cybersecurity, and
  possible government adoption.
- **Scientific honesty:** we do not claim "first in the world" without formal prior-art analysis;
  we distinguish detection vs quality vs verification vs identification vs enrollment; we never
  present screening numbers as a benchmark.
